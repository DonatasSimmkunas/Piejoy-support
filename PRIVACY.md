# Piejoy Privacy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Piejoy reads photos or screenshots only when you choose them. Text recognition happens on your iPhone. Recipes, grocery items, checked items, and your preferred store-section order are saved on your device and are not sent to a Piejoy server. The app does not require an account, show ads, or use analytics SDKs.

If you choose to share a recipe or grocery list, the iPhone share sheet sends its text to the messaging app, other service, or person you select. That service processes the shared text according to its own privacy policy. Piejoy does not share recipes or shopping lists automatically.

The free recipe count is stored in your device Keychain. Apple processes monthly and yearly subscriptions and makes their status available to the app through StoreKit; Piejoy does not receive payment card details. You can delete saved recipes in the app. Removing the app removes its local recipe files; the Keychain free-use record may remain on that device.

The included dish library is bundled with the app and works offline. No external AI service receives the name of a dish you search for.

For privacy questions or support, contact donatas@edvconsult.no. If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send so that we can respond. Please do not send private recipe photographs, identity documents, passwords or payment details. Apple handles purchases under its own privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.
